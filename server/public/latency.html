Handler · never leave the caller in dead air, never answer half a question

With the Handler on, the Handler takes the caller's question first, then hands the LLM one complete question. For every piece the caller says, it acknowledges at once, plays a hold sound while the caller waits, and decides whether the caller has finished, waiting a little for more words if not. The LLM answers once, and the Handler steps aside when the answer starts. No dead air, and no answering half a question or talking over the caller.
